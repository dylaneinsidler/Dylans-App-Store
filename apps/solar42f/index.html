Dylan's Solar 42F

An unofficial tribute to Elta Music's SOLAR 42F drone ambient machine: the same panel, with every knob, button, switch and jack working, and the sound worked out live as you play.

  • Knobs: drag up and down (Shift for fine moves), or scroll. Double-click (double-tap) to put one back.
  • Drone voices: hold a pad bottom right (or keys 1 to 6), or press HOLD on the voice to keep it on. MUTE the five oscillators to make chords; MOD lets them follow the light on the round sensor (hover or press it).
  • Touch keyboard: the twelve plates play VCO A and B (keys A W S E D F T G Y H U J; Z and X for octaves). Higher up a plate means more pressure.
  • Patch cables: drag from one jack to another. Drag a plug off to move it, tap a cable to remove it. With nothing plugged in, jacks use the instrument's own connections (hover a jack to see which).
  • Effector: tap the cartridge slot to change cartridge; the switches beside it pick each channel's program. What X, Y and Z do is written along the bottom.
  • Joystick: the figure bottom left. It stays where you leave it; double-tap to center it.
  • Contact mic: tap the microphone in PREAMP to play your own sounds through it.

Everything you set is kept for next time.
