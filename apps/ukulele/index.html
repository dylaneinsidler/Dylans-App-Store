Baritone Ukulele

tuned D · G · B · E

The neck

Tap a spot to press it, and again to lift your finger. Tap a letter at the top to switch that string off.

Chords

Tap a chord to hear it, see how to play it, and put it on the neck.

Notes

Every note the neck can play. Uke music is written one octave above how it sounds (like guitar). That’s normal.

Fingers: 1 pointer · 2 middle · 3 ring · 4 pinky. O = play the string open, ✕ = don’t play it.
